    Questions

    Why did my recording stop in the middle of the night?

    Usually a phone call, another app that takes the microphone, or AirPods disconnecting. Keep the phone plugged in with Sleep Focus on. If something does interrupt it, your Lock Screen shows a Resume Recording button. Fix a stopped recording

    Where are my recordings, and how do I share them?

    Tap the download arrow on an episode to save that clip to the Files app. For the whole night, open Export Report to get a ZIP with the audio and spreadsheets you can send to your doctor. Exporting and sharing

    Does it work with my Apple Watch or another watch?

    Your phone does the recording. A watch adds heart rate, HRV, and blood oxygen through Apple Health or Health Connect, and Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, and most other watches can tap your wrist when you snore. Watches and wearables

    Can it tell my snoring apart from my partner's, a fan, or music?

    It hears everything in the room, so it can't tell two snorers apart. Put the phone closest to the person you're tracking. Steady sound like a fan or music counts as background, and you can play back any moment to check. How detection works

    Is it really free? What happens to my data?

    Yes, every feature is free, with no ads or subscription. There's no account, detection runs on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

    Can it detect sleep apnea?

    It flags pauses in your breathing and lets you play them back, which is useful to show a doctor. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. Breathing disruptions
