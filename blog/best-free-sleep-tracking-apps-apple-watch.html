Best Free Sleep Tracking Apps for Apple Watch (2026)

By Menelik Tucker · Updated 8 Nov 2026

Quick answer

Apple’s built-in Health app is free with an Apple Watch. It tracks your sleep stages, heart rate and breathing rate and scores each night, but it has no smart alarm and records no sound. I make Snore Timeline, the free app I’d add, with no subscription, no trial and nothing locked. It turns your watch data into a recovery score against your own baseline, wakes you in light sleep with wrist taps, and records snoring you can play back. It picks up watch nights on its own, and it records audio.

Key takeaways

  • Same data: each app turns the same watch data into its own score. I found no independent lab test of any of these scores.
  • Automatic tracking: five of the six apps track on their own for free. Sleep Cycle charges for it.
  • Free-tier catch: Pillow and Sleep Cycle make you start a Premium trial before the free version opens.

    Will sleep tracking drain the battery?

    Few reviews raise it: 95 of 2,768 recent sleep reviews I coded mention battery, mostly about rings. A SleepWatch reviewer (21 Jan 2026) went from 100% at bedtime to 27% by morning.

    Snore Timeline records on the iPhone, so a dead watch costs only the wrist readings. Keep the phone plugged in; recording uses about 3 to 5% an hour, per the FAQ.

    Wear a ring or a different watch?

    Snore Timeline reads any device that writes sleep to Apple Health. Whether the data gets there free depends on the maker:

    • Free: Garmin, with Connect open for the sync to run [33]. Google Health, the renamed Fitbit app. Polar, sleep times only [31].
    • Paid plan first: Oura Gen3 and Ring 4 (Gen2 is exempt) [29]. WHOOP, which stops collecting data without a membership [30]. Withings accounts made on or after 13 October 2026.
    • HRV gap: none of those writes HRV to Apple Health, so recovery uses what arrives, such as heart rate. An Apple Watch Series 8 or later writes all six.
    • Stays behind: daytime activity, strain and readiness stay in the maker’s app. Galaxy Watch4 and later don’t work with an iPhone [32].

    Membership prices are in sleep trackers with no subscription.

    The apps, one by one

    Snore Timeline

    Snore Timeline records on the iPhone and estimates stages from breathing sounds, so nights you charge the watch still get a score. Watch nights still show on the timeline without a recording.

    • Free tier: everything, including the Smart Wake Up Alarm, Snore Nudge, full-night audio and CSV export.
    • Price: none. No in-app purchases [7].
    • Privacy label: Data Not Collected.
    • Reviews: 40 of 41 US App Store reviews (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026) give 5 stars. 22 praise that it’s free with no ads or subscription, 19 talk about snoring, 12 call it easy to use and 6 mention sleep talking.
    • Watch: the free Watch app stops a recording and shows live heart rate and a morning dashboard. The alarm can wake you with taps alone.

    SleepWatch

    SleepWatch logs sleep from the watch’s motion and heart rate with no button, counting sleep that starts between 7 p.m. and 11:30 a.m. [10].

    • Free tier: automatic tracking, daily check-ins and lifestyle tags, with ads [9].
    • Price: Premium, $3.99 to $39.99. Adds the SleepWatch Score, guidance, remedy analysis and snore recording.
    • Privacy label: Data Linked to You: Health & Fitness, User Content.
    • Reviews: 60 of 178 sleep reviews (Jan 2025 to Nov 2026) mention price. 17 describe nights or naps it didn’t log.

    Pillow

    Pillow tracks from the watch for free with its own stage analysis or Apple’s [12]. Automatic mode can’t set an alarm or record audio [13].

    • Free tier: automatic tracking, stages and heart rate range, after a 7-day trial starts [15].
    • Price: Premium, $19.99 to $59.99. Adds detailed analysis, trends, smart wake-up and audio [14].
    • Privacy label: Data Linked to You: Identifiers, Usage Data, Diagnostics.
    • Reviews: 57 of 284 sleep reviews (Sep 2025 to Nov 2026) mention price. 26 report missed nights or naps.

    Sleep Cycle

    Sleep Cycle’s smart alarm is free on the iPhone and the watch, which tracks with its accelerometer [17] [18].

    • Free tier: smart alarm, sleep analysis and a nightly graph, after you start a trial [19].
    • Price: Premium, $2.99 to $57.99. Adds automatic watch tracking, heart rate and snore detection.
    • Privacy label: Data Used to Track You: Identifiers.
    • Reviews: 103 of 326 sleep reviews (Mar 2025 to Nov 2026) are about the alarm, and 95 about price.

    Bevel

    Bevel reads your sleep from Apple Health and builds recovery, strain and stress scores around it [21].

    • Free tier: recovery, sleep, strain, stress, the energy bank and the Watch app [21].
    • Price: Pro, $14.99 a month or $99.99 a year, for the AI coach. AI credits cost $4.99 to $49.99.
    • Privacy label: Data Used to Track You: Identifiers. Health & Fitness is Not Linked.
    • Reviews: 45 of 78 sleep reviews (Sep 2025 to Nov 2026) talk about recovery. 24 describe sync trouble.
    • Watch: the free smart alarm needs an Apple Watch [22].

    Sleep++

    Sleep++ shows the stages, heart rate, breathing rate and blood oxygen your watch logs, plus a Readiness Score [24]. Version 4.8 dropped its Watch app and needs iOS 26.

    • Free tier: automatic tracking and the last 7 nights.
    • Price: One-time, $1.99, added in October 2026 to unlock full history.
    • Privacy label: Data Not Linked to You.
    • Reviews: 13 of 50 sleep reviews (Jan 2025 to Nov 2026) mention cost.

    Which one to pick

    • Pick Snore Timeline if you want recovery, a wrist-tap smart alarm and snoring you can hear, with audio stages on nights the watch charges.
    • Pick Apple’s built-in Health app if you want tracking that starts on its own and sleep apnea notifications.
    • Pick Pillow if you want its own stage analysis of your watch data.
    • Pick Sleep Cycle if you want a free smart alarm timed from wrist motion on the watch.
    • Pick Bevel if you want strain, stress and training next to your sleep.

    No watch hears snoring; snoring apps for Apple Watch covers that.

    Medical note

    Snore Timeline analyzes audio. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. If a score or a recording worries you, take it to a doctor.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Apple Watch sleep tracking free?

    Yes. Apple charges nothing for it, and Apple’s built-in Health app lists what you get and what it leaves out.

    Do I need a third-party app if I have an Apple Watch?

    Not to track sleep, which Apple does for free. You need one for a light-sleep alarm, recovery against your own baseline, or the sound of the night.

    Why do two apps show different deep sleep from the same watch?

    Some show Apple’s stages and others run their own analysis. Deep sleep is the hardest stage for any tracker to measure, so the numbers differ most there.

    The watch died overnight. Is the night lost?

    For watch-only apps, yes. Snore Timeline records on the phone, so only the wrist readings are missing.
