How to Record Someone Snoring: For the Partner Who Can't Sleep

By Menelik Tucker · Updated 9 Oct 2026

Quick answer

Put a phone on the snorer’s side of the bed, one to two feet from their head, and record all night. In the morning, play the loudest episodes. A loud, full snore came from the person beside the phone, and a faint one came from across the bed. I make Snore Timeline, which does this with nothing to buy and lets you send any episode as a clip.

Key takeaways

  • Two sleepers: of the apps compared here, only Sleep Cycle tries to tell two snorers apart, and its Who’s Snoring feature needs Premium [3]. SnoreLab’s FAQ says SnoreLab can’t [4].
  • Placement: the phone hears whoever is closest, so put it on the snorer’s side.
  • Gasping: a silent pause that ends in a choke or gasp is a sign of sleep apnea, and worth taking to a doctor [1].

    Where should the phone go?

    The app records whatever the microphone hears, so placement decides whose snoring you get.

    1. Put it on their side of the bed, one to two feet from their head.
    2. Plug it in and lay it face down. Recording uses about 5 to 7 percent of the battery per hour.
    3. Turn off fans and white noise. They cover the breathing the app listens for.
    4. Set the app’s sensitivity to Minimal if you snore too. Minimal keeps only loud snoring, and the loudest snorer tends to be whoever is nearest the phone.

    Whose snore is it?

    1. Open the episode list in the morning and start with the loudest.
    2. Play it. A loud, full snore came from the person beside the phone. A faint one came from across the bed.

    If you both snore, record two nights with the phone on each side in turn.

    How do I share the clip?

    1. Tap the download icon next to an episode. The clip saves to the Files app.
    2. Tap the share icon and send it by Messages, email or AirDrop.

    For a doctor, use Export Report. It saves a spreadsheet of every sound plus the full night’s audio. The export page has the steps.

    When should the recording go to a doctor?

    If the snoring stops, goes silent, and comes back with a choke or a gasp, play the clip for your partner and book a doctor’s appointment together [1] [2].

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I record my partner snoring?

    Put the phone on their side of the bed and start recording before you sleep. In the morning, play the loudest episodes to check whose snoring it is.

    Can an app tell which of us is snoring?

    Of the apps compared here, only Sleep Cycle tries, with its Who’s Snoring feature, which needs Premium. With any other app, put the phone on the snorer’s side and judge by how loud each snore sounds.

    My partner gasps between snores. What should I do with the recording?

    Play it for your partner and book a doctor’s appointment together. Bring the clip or the full night’s export to the appointment.
