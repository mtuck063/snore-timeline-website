How to Record Someone Snoring: For the Partner Who Can't Sleep

By Menelik Tucker · Updated 9 Oct 2026

Quick answer

Put a phone on the snorer’s side of the bed, one to two feet from their head, and record all night. In the morning, play the loudest episodes: loud and full is beside the phone, faint is across the bed. I make Snore Timeline, which does this with nothing to buy and lets you send any episode as a clip.

Key takeaways

  • Two sleepers: only Sleep Cycle tries to tell them apart, and its Who’s Snoring needs Premium [3]. SnoreLab’s FAQ says it can’t [4].
  • Placement: the phone hears whoever is closest, so put it on the snorer’s side.
  • Gasping: silent pauses followed by a choke or gasp are a sign of sleep apnea worth taking to a doctor [1].

    Where should the phone go?

    The app records whatever the microphone hears, so placement decides whose snoring you get.

    1. Put it on their side of the bed, one to two feet from their head.
    2. Plug it in and lay it face down. A night of recording uses 5 to 7 percent of battery an hour.
    3. Turn off fans and white noise. They cover the breathing the app listens for.
    4. Set sensitivity to Minimal if you snore too. It keeps only loud snoring, which tends to be whoever is nearest the phone.

    Whose snore is it?

    1. Open the episode list in the morning and start with the loudest.
    2. Play it. Loud and full means beside the phone; faint means across the bed.

    If you both snore, record two nights with the phone on each side in turn.

    How do I share the clip?

    1. Tap the download icon next to an episode. The clip saves to the Files app.
    2. Tap the share icon and send it by Messages, email or AirDrop.

    For a doctor, Export Report gives a spreadsheet of every sound and the full night’s audio. The export page has the steps.

    When should the recording go to a doctor?

    If the snoring stops into silence and comes back as a choke or a gasp, play it for them and book an appointment together [1] [2].

    Snore Timeline analyzes audio on your phone. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. A breathing disruption on the timeline is a sound pattern to show a doctor, and quiet events make no sound at all. If the person you recorded gasps, chokes or stops breathing between snores, or is tired through the day, take the recording to a healthcare provider.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I record my partner snoring?

    Put the phone on their side of the bed and start recording before you sleep. In the morning, play the loudest episodes to check whose snoring it is.

    Can an app tell which of us is snoring?

    Only Sleep Cycle tries, with its Premium Who’s Snoring feature. With any other app, put the phone on the snorer’s side and judge by how loud each snore sounds.

    My partner gasps between snores. What should I do with the recording?

    Play it for them and book a doctor together. Bring the clip or the full night’s export to the appointment.
